The label above a field, with optional "Optional" marker and info icon. Used inside **Form Field**.

---

**Properties**
- **Type** — `Vertical` · `Horizontal`
- **Show/hide** — Tooltip, Link, Optional, Counter, Required

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Use short noun phrases ("Email address").

---

**Accessibility**
- Must be a real `<label>` connected to its field.
