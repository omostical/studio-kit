**Properties**
- **Type** — `Button` · `Area`
- **State** — `Default` · `Hover` · `Pressed` · `Focused` · `Disabled`

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Highlight the area while a file is dragged over it.
- Always include a "Browse" button for people who can't drag.

---

**Accessibility**
- The browse button is the accessible control; the drop area is an enhancement.
