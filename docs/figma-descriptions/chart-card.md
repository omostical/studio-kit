A card that holds one chart with its title, summary figures and legend, for dashboards.

---

**Properties**
- **Container** — `True` · `False`
- **Show/hide** — Header, Footer, Description

---

**Usage guidelines**
- One chart per card, with a title that says what it shows ("Revenue, last 30 days").
- Show the key number in text, not only in the chart.

---

**Accessibility**
- Provide the data in text or a table for people who can't see the chart.
- Don't rely on colour alone to tell series apart: use labels or patterns too.
