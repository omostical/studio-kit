**Status: planned** — Figma only; there is no code component yet.

One editable cell of a spreadsheet: text, number, select, date and other types.

---

**Properties**
- **Type** — `Empty` · `Header →` · `Text` · `Checkbox` · `Status Badge` · `Tags` · `Switch` · `Progress` · `Icon` · `Person` · `Thumbnail` · `Number` · `Payment Method`
- **Size** — `Small` · `Medium`
- **State** — `Default` · `Hover` · `Pressed`
- **Selected** — `False` · `True`
- **Show/hide** — Chevron

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Show validation errors on the cell itself.

---

**Accessibility**
- Each cell is a `gridcell`; the editing control inside follows its own component's guidance.
