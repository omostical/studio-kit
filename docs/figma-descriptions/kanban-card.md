A compact card for one item on a board (task, deal, ticket), or a group of them. Use it on board columns, not as a general container.

---

**Properties**
- **Type** — `Single` · `Group`
- **Show/hide** — Details, Description, Status, Attachments, Tags, Badges, Comments, Date, Avatar

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Show only what people scan for: title, owner, status, date.
- Open the full item in a **Side Panel** or page.

---

**Accessibility**
- If cards can be dragged, also offer a keyboard or menu way to move them.
