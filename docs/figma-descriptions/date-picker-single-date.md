A field for one date, with a **Calendar** popover. Use **Date picker (Date range)** for start and end dates.

---

**Properties**
- **Variant** — `Primary` · `Danger`
- **Style** — `Border` · `Ghost`
- **Size** — `Medium` · `Small` · `Large`
- **State** — `Default` · `Hover` · `Pressed` · `Active` · `Disabled`
- **Filled** — `True` · `False`
- **Show/hide** — Icon
- **Text** — Label

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Always allow typing the date as well as picking it; show the expected format.
- For dates far in the past (birth dates), typing or separate day/month/year fields work better than a calendar.

---

**Accessibility**
- The field follows Input guidance; the calendar button needs a label ("Choose date").
- The calendar follows the Calendar keyboard pattern and returns focus to the field when closed.
