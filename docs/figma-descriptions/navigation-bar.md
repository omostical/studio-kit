The top bar of the app: product logo, main navigation, search and account. Use **Sidebar** when there are many sections.

---

**Properties**
- **Type** — `Navigation` · `Breadcrumbs`
- **Show/hide** — Search, Segmented Control, Menu Button

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Keep top-level links to about 5–7.
- Highlight the current section.

---

**Accessibility**
- Wrap links in `<nav>` with a label, and mark the current link with `aria-current="page"`.
- Provide a "Skip to content" link before it.
