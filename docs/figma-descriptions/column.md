A table column with its header and **Table Cell** items, for building tables of structured data.

---

**Properties**
- **Type** — `Select` · `Switch` · `Status Badge` · `Icon` · `Progress` · `Text Input` · `Checkbox` · `Actions` · `Tags` · `Text` · `Person` · `Thumbnail` · `Payment Method` · `Number` · `Empty`
- **Divider** — `False` · `True`

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Left-align text and right-align numbers.
- Let people sort columns where it's useful and show the sort direction.

---

**Accessibility**
- Use real `<table>` markup with `<th scope="col">`; sortable headers are buttons with `aria-sort`.
