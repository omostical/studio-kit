A building block that shows a person or object with an avatar or thumbnail, title and secondary text, used in lists, tables and menus.

---

**Properties**
- **Type** — `Icon - Subtle` · `Icon - Base` · `Icon - Strong` · `Avatar` · `Thumbnail` · `Country Flag` · `Payment` · `Logo` · `Icon - Danger`
- **Size** — `16px` · `20px` · `24px` · `32px`
- **Swappable** — Logo Type, Swap Icon

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Keep the secondary line short and useful (email, role, size).
