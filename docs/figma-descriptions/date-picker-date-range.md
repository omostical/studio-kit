A field for a start and end date, with a two-month **Calendar** popover and optional preset ranges ("Last 7 days").

---

**Properties**
- **Size** — `Medium` · `Small` · `Large`

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Offer presets for common ranges.
- Prevent end dates before the start date and explain any limits.

---

**Accessibility**
- Label both dates ("Start date", "End date") and announce the selected range.
