A small read-only label for categories and metadata (project type, team, topic). Use **Status Badge** for states and **Chip** for interactive filters or values.

---

**Properties**
- **Variant** — `Label` · `Icon` · `Label + Icon`
- **Color** — `Brand` · `Red` · `Pink` · `Violet` · `Purple` · `Indigo` · `Blue` · `Cyan` · `Teal` · `Green` · `Lime` · `Yellow` · `Orange`
- **Emphasis** — `Filled` · `Border`
- **Size** — `Large` · `Medium` · `Small`
- **Rounded** — `False` · `True`
- **Text** — Label
- **Swappable** — Swap Icon

---

**Usage guidelines**
- Use colours for categories only; they don't carry meaning like success or danger.
- Keep labels short.

---

**Accessibility**
- The text must stand on its own; colour is decoration.
